TO MY DEAREST KUCHUUU...

|
Forever yours, Your Puchu ♡

SEALED WITH A THOUSAND KISSES

My dearest Kuchuuu, Do you know something, baby? I've never really understood why the world needs a particular day to celebrate a princess. I mean, what about all the other days? What about the sleepy mornings, the random afternoons, the quiet nights, the days when you're laughing uncontrollably, and even those days when you don't feel like smiling at all? Do you stop being someone's princess on those days? Not mine, sweetheart. Never mine. You see, you aren't my princess because a calendar says so. You aren't my princess because today is special, because you're dressed beautifully, because you're smiling, or because everything around you is going perfectly. You're my princess simply because you're you. And no single day, occasion, mood, mistake, distance or difficult moment could ever decide your worth in my eyes. I wish you could see yourself through my eyes sometimes. Not just when you're all dressed up and feeling pretty, but when you're sleepy, when you're being a little silly, when you're talking nonsense, when you're overthinking, when you're quiet, and even when you don't particularly like yourself. I'd still look at you and think, there she is, my favourite girl. And I hope you never feel that you have to be a certain version of yourself to be loved by me. You don't have to be cheerful every time we talk. You don't have to hide your little worries or pretend that everything is okay. You can be vulnerable with me. You can be a little childish, a little dramatic, a little stubborn, a little clingy. You can have your bad days. You can simply exist beside me without having to prove anything. I don't want to love only the easy-to-love parts of you. I want to know the little things, too. The things you find difficult to explain, the thoughts you keep to yourself, the dreams you're hesitant to speak about, the insecurities you wish you didn't have. I want to understand you, not just admire you. And baby, I hope you know that being your Puchu isn't just about calling you pretty names, sending kisses or making you smile. It's also about choosing to be gentle with your heart, being patient when things get difficult, listening when you need to be heard, and making sure you never feel that you have to compete for your place in my life. I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. We are two imperfect little humans, after all. There will be misunderstandings, moods, silly arguments and moments when we won't know exactly what to say. But I hope we always remember that we're on the same side. I'd rather understand you than win an argument. I'd rather work through a difficult moment than let silence make us strangers. And if there's one thing I want you to carry with you, it's this: You never have to wait for a special day to feel special to me. On a random Wednesday, when you're doing absolutely nothing, you're my princess. On a difficult Monday, when you don't feel pretty or confident, you're my princess. On the days when life feels a little too heavy, you're still my princess. And on all those ordinary days that nobody celebrates, I'll still find a thousand little reasons to adore you. I wish I could sometimes pause time, pull you a little closer, kiss your forehead, brush your hair away from your face and tell you how much you mean to me. Maybe I'd hold your hand for a while and let you talk about absolutely anything. Or maybe we'd just sit quietly, because not every beautiful moment needs words. And then, of course, I'd probably annoy you with kisses until you started laughing. Muwahhhhh! My poor princess would have absolutely no royal authority over that. Hehe. Thank you for being my Kuchuuu. Thank you for all the little moments, the laughter, the affection, the conversations, the silliness and the memories we're still making. I don't take the little things lightly. Sometimes, the smallest moments with you become the ones I carry around in my heart throughout the day. I hope I get to keep discovering you. Not just the girl I fell for, but all the versions of you that life will bring along. I hope we grow, learn, become better for ourselves and keep finding our way back to each other. So here's a little reminder from your Puchu: Your crown doesn't disappear when you have a bad day. Your worth doesn't change when you make a mistake. You don't become less precious when you're upset, tired or uncertain. And you never need an occasion to deserve tenderness. You are my princess on the days the world celebrates you, and on the days the world doesn't even notice. Today, tomorrow, on all the ordinary days, and in all the little moments in between. Now come here, Kuchuuu. One forehead kiss, two cheek kisses, a very tight hug, and approximately a million more muwahhhhhssshshshs. I love you, my girl. Forever your Puchu. ♡
